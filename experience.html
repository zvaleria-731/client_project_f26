Sharing Your Engaged Learning Experiences!

Learn from Each Other, Share Your Experience

Sharing your engaged learning experience gives other students an opportunity to learn from your experiences, perspectives, and lessons learned. By sharing what you worked on, what you found valuable or challenging, and how the experience connected to your goals, you can help peers understand what a program is really like beyond its formal description. These firsthand insights can help students set realistic expectations, discover opportunities they may not have considered, and determine whether a program aligns with their interests, learning styles, and career goals. Hearing from students who have already participated can make it easier for others to make informed decisions about how they want to spend their time and which opportunities are worthwhile for them.

Sharing is also an opportunity to reflect on your own experience. Putting your thoughts into words can help you recognize what you learned, identify the skills and personal growth you gained, and articulate the value of your experience. Your perspective can also help build a stronger student community by connecting people with similar interests, encouraging conversations, and creating opportunities for future collaboration and support. By sharing what you have learned, you are not only reflecting on your own journey—you are helping the next student navigate theirs.
